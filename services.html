SOLUTIONS & OFFERINGS

Fifteen integrated health services designed for comprehensive care.

Everything you need in one place: verified doctor telehealth consultations, precision diagnostic testing, smart hardware, affordable generic pharmacy, and emergency safety.

01 · Clinical Care & Doctor Telehealth

Consult top physicians, cardiologists, endocrinologists, and specialists over encrypted HD video calls.

  • Search doctors by specialty, spoken language, consultation fee, and patient ratings.
  • Doctors review your continuous watch telemetry during the consultation.
  • Instant digital prescription attached permanently to your health ID.

Structured e-prescriptions generated immediately post-consultation with medication names, exact dosages, and diet instructions.

  • Lifetime encrypted storage compliant with Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM).
  • Prevents adverse drug-to-drug interactions through automated AI safety screening.
  • Instantly connects to pharmacy order fulfillment and daily pill reminders.
02 · Diagnostics & AI Lab Report Scanner

Certified phlebotomists collect blood and urine samples right at your doorstep for 100+ diagnostic parameters.

  • Full Body Checkup, Lipid Panel, HbA1c, Thyroid Profile, and Vitamin D3/B12.
  • Digital test results delivered within 12 to 24 hours.
  • Automatic synchronization of biomarkers to your SAVIX digital twin.

Upload any diagnostic lab PDF or paper scan to receive an instant, color-coded plain-language breakdown.

  • Visual indicators comparing biomarkers against standard clinical reference ranges.
  • Translates complex medical acronyms into actionable clinical insights.
  • Generates personalized doctor discussion points for your next follow-up.
03 · Smart Wearables & Hardware

An ultra-affordable medical sensor watch engineered for continuous, zero-friction health tracking.

  • Continuous Heart Rate, SpO2, HRV, and sleep stage tracking.
  • Autonomous background sync over BLE & Cellular networks.
  • 7-day battery life with water resistance up to 50 meters.
04 · Generic Pharmacy & Emergency SOS

Discovers the nearest Jan Aushadhi generic equivalents to save up to 70% on monthly medicine expenditures.

  • Identifies identical chemical salt formulations at guaranteed subsidized rates.
  • Real-time stock availability in nearby participating pharmacies.

Activated via a button on the watch, a triple shake, or voice command "Help" to trigger simultaneous rapid dispatch.

  • Dispatches GPS coordinates, blood group, allergies, and emergency contacts to 108/112 ambulance services.
  • Simultaneously auto-dials family contacts with automated voice assistance.

Automated engine matching you and your family to available national and state public health subsidies.

  • Instant qualification check for Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) and state-specific welfare programs.
  • Step-by-step paperless application guides and authorized hospital empanelment lists.
05 — Financials, Schemes & Multilingual Support

Identify relevant health-related government schemes based on your eligibility profile to maximize benefits.

  • Explain benefits, eligibility, and provide notifications.
  • Direct links to official information and application processes.

Track medicine, consultation, hospitalization, and treatment expenses. Set monthly healthcare savings targets to build a financial buffer for medical emergencies.

Medical reports and terminology are often in English. SAVIX_AI translates and explains healthcare information in supported Indian languages.

  • Supports Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, and more.
  • Localized app interface to improve accessibility for everyone.
